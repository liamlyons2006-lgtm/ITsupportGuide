## Before you start

- Have the ticket queue open and ready to log a new ticket.
- Take a breath. The first fifteen seconds set the tone for the whole contact.

## Steps

1. Greet, give your name, and name the team: "Hello, you're through to IT
   Support, this is Alex."
2. Confirm who you are speaking to and verify identity per your organisation's
   procedure.
3. Invite the problem with an open question: "What can I help you with today?"
4. Let them explain without interrupting. Acknowledge: "Got it, that sounds
   frustrating."
5. Signpost what happens next: "I'll take a look now and talk you through it."
6. For chat, keep the same shape but shorter, and say when you step away: "Give me
   a moment to check that."

:::tip
Mirror the user's words back. If they say "the thing won't turn on", repeat
"the thing won't turn on" before translating it into technical terms. It shows
you listened.
:::

## Verify it worked

- The user has confirmed their identity and described the problem.
- The user knows your name and what you are about to do.
- The tone is calm and the ticket is open.
